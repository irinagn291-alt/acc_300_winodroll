Three behaviours are deliberate and can look like a defect on a fast pass. Please check these first.

1. On the Stamp sheet, "Stamp" stays disabled until Name is filled, Price is above zero, Priority is above zero, and Necessity, 0 to 100 and Discretion, 0 to 100 sit in range. Complete those fields and the button enables. Leaving mid-edit shows "Discard this want?" / "The name and figures are not stamped." — choose "Discard" or "Keep".

2. On Rules, "Save" stays disabled until Monthly impulse limit is above zero. Enter a positive limit, then tap Save. An unsaved draft shows "Discard the limit?" / "The new limit is not saved." with "Discard" or "Keep".

3. In Cut, tapping a Short row shows "Cover is short. Ticket stays." That is intentional. Pick a row marked Cover, or wait the rail out. "Bought" and "Dropped" replace "Cut" on the desk only when remaining hours reach zero; trying to release early without a cover can alert "Hold refused" with "Wait or cut." and "OK".

Technical facts: no accounts and no in-app purchases. The tracking permission is the system dialog on a cold launch, before the desk. Its text is "We use this identifier to personalize your experience and measure app performance." It is shown once per install, and only when Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > Allow Apps to Request to Track is on. If that switch is off, iOS does not present it — turn it on, delete Winodroll, and install again. Desk data stays on this device. UI strings are English; numbers follow the device locale. Portrait only, iPhone and iPad, iOS 17.0 or later.
