Winodroll is a local will-call desk for impulse shoppers. You stamp a priced want onto a hold ticket, wait out computed hours on a live rail, then mark it bought or dropped—or cut early by sacrificing another cooling ticket.

• Stamp a priced want with Name, Price, Priority, Necessity, 0 to 100, Discretion, 0 to 100, and Retail code, optional
• Live hour rail (Hold this purchase) with hours left, stamped, covers, and limit
• Monthly impulse limit that sets hold hours from price versus this limit, then priority, necessity, and discretion
• Hold labels Blank, Cooling, Released, Released, bought, Released, dropped, and Released, cut
• Same retail code keeps the longer span without restarting the rail
• Cut early by covering with another cooling ticket; Short cover leaves the face ticket in place
• Bought and Dropped when remaining hours reach zero
• Review of Released, Cut, and Cooling tickets
• Rules with Monthly impulse limit and Hours at this limit
• Longer span sheet; Settings with Show the intro, Reset the desk, and Contact
• Local only. No checkout.

Everything stays on this device. Portrait only. Requires iOS 17.0 or later.
