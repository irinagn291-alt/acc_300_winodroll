Winodroll is a local will-call desk for impulse shoppers. You stamp a priced want onto a hold ticket, wait out computed hours on a live rail, then mark it bought or dropped—or cut early by sacrificing another cooling ticket.

• Stamp a priced want (name, price, priority, necessity, discretion, optional retail code) onto a hold ticket
• Live hour rail with remaining hours, "hours left", and captions "stamped", "covers", and "limit"
• Monthly impulse limit that sets hold hours from price versus the limit, then priority, necessity, and discretion
• Hold labels "Blank", "Cooling", "Released", "Released, bought", "Released, dropped", and "Released, cut"
• Same retail code keeps the longer span without restarting the rail
• Cut early by covering with another cooling ticket’s remaining hours; a Short cover leaves the ticket in place
• "Bought" and "Dropped" when remaining hours reach zero
• Review of Released, Cut, and Cooling tickets
• Rules board for "Monthly impulse limit" and "Hours at this limit"
• Longer span sheet on how hours are set and how early cut works
• Settings: "Show the intro", "Reset the desk", and Contact
• Everything stays on this device; "Local only. No checkout."

Portrait only. Dark appearance. Requires iOS 17.0 or later.
