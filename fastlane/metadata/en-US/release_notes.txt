Version 1.0 introduces Winodroll, a local will-call desk for impulse shoppers.

• Intro pages Hold the purchase, The rail ticks hours, and Same code keeps longer
• Desk with Review, Rules, Settings, Stamp, Cut, Bought, and Dropped
• Stamp sheet for Name, Price, Priority, Necessity, 0 to 100, Discretion, 0 to 100, and Retail code, optional
• Live hour rail with Hold this purchase, hours left, stamped, covers, and limit
• Cut sheet with Cover and Short cooling tickets
• Longer span sheet for same retail code and cut-by-cover rules
• Review of Released, Cut, and Cooling
• Rules for Monthly impulse limit and Hours at this limit
• Settings with Contact, Show the intro, and Reset the desk
• Desk data stays on this device; Local only. No checkout
